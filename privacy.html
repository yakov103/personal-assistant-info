Privacy information

This is an owner-operated personal-use assistant, not a service offered to the public. This information describes its intended data use.

Access and purpose

Google access is limited to the permissions the owner grants: reading email, sending approved email, reading and changing approved calendar events, reading Drive files and creating or uploading approved files. The assistant uses selected content to fulfill the owner’s requests and agreed briefings.

Storage and processing

Assistant state, selected memories and Google credentials are kept in private files on the owner’s encrypted Ubuntu system disk. Encrypted backups may be stored on the owner’s Mac. Selected content may be processed by the owner’s configured model provider and delivered through the chosen messaging service; those services’ privacy terms and account settings also apply.

Google user data is not sold or used by this assistant for advertising. Access is restricted to the owner. Source data and selected memory remain until removed by the owner or by configured retention. This site itself has no forms, analytics or access to the assistant’s records; the hosting provider may keep normal service logs.

Control and contact

The owner can revoke the Google connection in their Google Account, stop the local assistant and remove locally stored data or backups. Contact the operator through the yakov103 GitHub profile.
