Personal Assistant

A private personal assistant operated by the owner of the yakov103 GitHub account. It helps that owner work with their email, calendar, files, reminders and selected personal tasks.

The assistant runs on the owner’s Ubuntu computer. The owner communicates through their approved Telegram and WhatsApp accounts. This website contains information only: it has no sign-in form, account registration, assistant dashboard or access to personal records.

Google connection

The owner authorizes selected Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive permissions through Google’s own consent screen. Email sends, calendar changes and file uploads require the owner’s approval. No public sign-ups are offered.
